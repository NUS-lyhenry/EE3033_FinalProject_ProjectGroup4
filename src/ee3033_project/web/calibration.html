ONE MEASUREMENT · AUTOMATIC COLLECTION

Calibrate with one fixed bottle

Place one upright bottle ahead of the camera on a flat floor. Leave it in exactly the same place throughout. The robot will centre it, move backward up to 0.72 m, collect different views, then return partway for two checks.

Measure the straight floor distance from the point directly below the camera lens to the centre of the bottle base, at the robot’s current position. This can be diagonal; no left offset or camera height measurement is needed. Start immediately after measuring.

What this calibration covers
Effective camera geometry for upright floor targets on the same level, within the collected range. Automatic checks use held-out robot poses; their accuracy depends on odometry and localization and is not an independently surveyed accuracy guarantee. Keep the lens settings and mounting unchanged. Bottle outlines approximate its geometry; a checkerboard is better for full lens/distortion calibration. Camera pitch changes while balancing, floor slope, and map/robot pose errors can still affect reported coordinates.
During assessment stage 2, use the saved profile autonomously. No measured target positions from this session are used as mission coordinates.
